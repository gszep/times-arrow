What is this?

Independent particles move left or right on a ring, one site per step: this is the model’s light speed. Each reverses independently with probability 1/64 per step by default. Two smooth bumps are prepared above a stationary Poisson background with one particle per link on average.

“Information left” is the relative entropy between the prepared state and equilibrium, not the entropy of a single trajectory. Equilibrium itself shows no arrow here. The colours show information on a logarithmic scale; gold curve segments fall and red segments rise.

The gas has a rest frame, that of the scatterers. Observers agree on the direction of the fall, not on its rate: the model is not Lorentz invariant. “Clock runs backwards” in the caption means information increases, not that a physical clock reverses.

The wall statement is about all states and walls that things can cross. A reflecting wall, or a state with nothing outside, also never leaks. The region’s other end retreats at light speed too, beyond this diagram; both ends must prevent inflow. Faster-than-light retreat is a choice of region, not a moving material wall.

Lean proves the general theorem and its deterministic converse for finite lattice dynamics in CausalEntropy.lean, not this Poisson gas. Here exact ensemble formulas are evaluated in floating point: f32 evolution on the GPU, checked against an independent f64 evaluation; the information sums use f64. These are numerical checks, not certified error bounds.

The continuum connection is the “information current” of Gavassino, Antonelli and Haskell, PRL 128, 010606 (2022), for perturbations near relativistic equilibrium; this lattice illustration is not a continuum-limit test.

The ring has 2048 sites and 768 steps. We show space within 200 sites of the centre and the first 400 steps. For deviation d on a link, information is φ(d) = (1+d) ln(1+d) − d. Observer cuts sum links crossing t − u(x−c) = s, only for complete cuts; walls sum c + ⌊vt⌋ ≤ x ≤ c + 900 − t, for t = 0…399. The perturbation never wraps around the ring.

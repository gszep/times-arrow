Clock rate Φ per area vs radius r

Φ(r) = mean entropy production per window of the two-cell partition "inside the circle/square of radius r vs the rest", divided by the window length and the region area; r is the equivalent radius √(area/π), so circle and equal-area square share an x position. Noisy at small r, a plateau if the arrow is a smooth density, geometry-dominated at large r — that is the conjecture under test. Bands: ±2 standard errors over the seed ensemble.

Backward windows: P(σ<0) vs r

The fraction of windows in which the region ran its clock backward. At equilibrium every region sits at ½; during a leak the smallest regions should still fluctuate backward while the large ones cannot. Change W (window length) above and watch P move.

Fluctuation check: P(σ<0) vs ⟨σ⟩/sd

All 28 surfaces on one axis: the measured backward fraction against each region's signal-to-noise ratio ⟨σ⟩/sd, with the Gaussian tail ½·erfc(x/√2) dashed. If the per-window fluctuations are roughly Gaussian, all families fall on the curve whatever their r (exploratory reading of the fluctuation-theorem expectation).

Definitions and assumptions
  • 2D billiards in the unit square: continuous f32 positions, specular infinitely thin walls; N non-interacting point particles, one speed (0.02 widths/step) or a ±25% spread; up to 9 fixed circular scatterers; initial condition drawn by Philox4x32-10 keyed (seed, step, site). The seed is in the URL.
  • Coarse cells: a C×C grid over the table (C = 32 or 64). Per window of W steps the GPU tallies, as integers, every directed crossing of every internal cell edge (attributed to the cell being left, the crossing point fixing the orthogonal index) and takes a census at the window end: per-cell occupancy and, for each of the 28 surfaces, the count inside.
  • Per edge e between neighbouring cells c and e': J_e = (crossings c→e') − (crossings e'→c), and the edge's entropy production is σ_e = (J_e/N)·ln((n_c+½)/(n_e'+½)) — nats per particle over the window, densities n/A with a ½-particle pseudo-count so empty cells stay finite (cells have equal area). The cell value σ_c sums its four edges; this is the volume-weighted choice consistent with S_obs = −D(p‖a) for a two-cell partition. The window-start census supplies the densities.
  • ∇·J at a cell is the raw net outflow Σ_e J_e (crossings per window). Σ over all cells it is 0 exactly (every crossing leaves one cell and enters another) — the live line asserts it.
  • Region clock: for surface k, p(t) = inside count / N and σ_k(window) = S_obs(p_end, a_k) − S_obs(p_start, a_k) with S_obs = −p ln(p/a) − (1−p) ln((1−p)/(1−a)) per particle; Φ = ⟨σ_k⟩/W. Regions may be clipped by table walls: square areas are exact, clipped circle areas use a 4096² supersample (error ≲ 1e-3) — tap near a wall to make one.
  • The seed ensemble re-runs the same scenario over R seeds (same spawn stream shifted by seed) and reports mean ± 2 standard errors of the per-seed values; a single run can go against the trend.
  • What would kill the readings shown here: census sums ≠ N, Σ∇·J ≠ 0 (live line), door tunnelling with the door shut, or speed drift outside the spread toggle.

Coarse entropy per surface — volume-weighted S_obs, faint unweighted S

S_obs = −p ln(p/a) − (1−p) ln((1−p)/(1−a)) per particle (equivalently −D(p‖a) on the unit table): maximal 0 exactly at equilibrium p = a. The faint grey dashed curve is the old unweighted S(p) for surface 0 — it peaks at p = 1/2, which is not equilibrium when a ≠ 1/2.

Spectrum of surface 0 after the switch (or release)

Dashed ticks: the table's geometric times, periods 100/√(m²+n²) steps for m²+n² = 1…8 (a particle conserves |v_x|, |v_y|, so its x and y motions are periodic with periods 2/|v_x| and 2/|v_y|; the slowest is 2L/v = 100 steps).

Assumptions
  • 2D billiards in the unit square: continuous f32 positions, no lattice. Free-flight speed 0.02 table widths per step, one speed for all particles (or a ±spread toggle); specular, infinitely thin walls; up to 9 fixed circular scatterers of one total area.
  • N non-interacting point particles; initial condition drawn by Philox4x32-10 keyed (seed, step, site) — the seed is in the URL. Protocol A starts uniform inside the room; protocol B releases from a disc (or point) at the table centre with uniform directions. Counts are sampled once per chunk for every counting surface simultaneously.
  • Reverse negates every velocity; specular reflection and the wall snap are time-symmetric, so the run retraces its own history up to f32 rounding.
  • The ensemble mean over R seeds keeps the part of the signal phase-locked to the protocol (same switch time for every seed) and suppresses seed noise like 1/√R; the band is ±2 standard errors of the mean. A single run can go against the trend.
  • S_obs is a relative entropy, not a thermodynamic entropy: it is 0 at the observed p = a and negative away from it, and rises monotonically toward equilibrium only as long as |p − a| keeps shrinking.
  • What would kill the reading shown here: wall tunnelling (watch N_in with the door shut), speed drift outside the stated spread, or aligned counts that stop descending during the walls-on leak.

| Q3 | Do thermodynamic speed limits (entropy-production bounds on how fast a state can change) and Lieb–Robinson light cones (bounds on how fast information can spread) unify into one bound? | open. 003 supports the composed bound `W₁ ≤ min(t, E_diss)`, which is the smaller of two known bounds, not a unification. | Q4 | Can the orientation of the light cone (which half is the future), and not only the flow of time, be derived from the statistical state? Not from causality alone: for reversible lattice dynamics the causal second law holds in both time directions (proved, `causal_second_law_reverse`) | [001](../experiments/001-irreversibility/) (concluded), [002](../experiments/002-arrow-kl/) (concluded), [#11](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/11), [#14](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/14) |
| Q5 | Is there a transformation theory between system/environment partitions, the thermodynamic analogue of the Lorentz group? What is invariant under it (total entropy) and what transforms (entropy production, heat, temperature)? | open. Desk answer: partitions form a lattice with a one-way coarsening semigroup, not a group (conjecture, #8). Which changes keep the arrow: for a fixed dynamics and pair of regions, relative entropy is non-increasing for all states iff the later region's state is a function of the earlier region's (proved, `TimesArrow/CausalEntropy.lean`) | [001](../experiments/001-irreversibility/) (concluded), [006 · #8](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/8), [#14](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/14) |
| Q6 | When is a change of reference frame the same as a change of partition? Unruh is one case. Is it general? | open. Desk answer: only when the partition boundary is the horizon of the frame's own flow (literature and derived, #11). On a lattice: a slicing always keeps the arrow, a wall only when nothing can enter (#14) | [007 · #11](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/11), [#14](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/14) |
| Q7 | Can proper time be defined as the tick count of a dissipative clock, consistently with Lorentz covariance? Do clock-accuracy bounds (TURs) become bounds on how precisely proper time can be resolved? | open | [005 · #10](https://github.com/gszep/times-arrow/issues/10) |

Hypothesis. The HPP gas is a reversible bijection with a strict light cone, so any arrow its states show must come from the initial condition and the partition, not the law: entropy rises from a packed block into the equilibrium band, the exact echo returns the state bit for bit, and one flipped bit destroys the reversal while the naive flip-only reversal fails to retrace.
